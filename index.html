Rahasya Kosh Publisher

A personal tool for the Rahasya Kosh channel, which makes Hindi story videos.

Rahasya Kosh Publisher is a small program that runs on the channel owner's own computer. It is not a public service, and nobody else can sign up for it.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and schedules when they go live.
  • Posts short videos as Reels to the owner's own Instagram account.
  • Reads the channel's own statistics (views, watch time, search terms, subscribers gained) so the owner can see which videos work best.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. It acts only on accounts that the owner signs in to and approves. It never reads other people's channels or accounts.

Your data

Everything the tool stores stays on the owner's computer. Read the full privacy policy for details.
